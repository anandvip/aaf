Anand Agro Farms

Pehowa · Kheri Shish Gram · District Kurukshetra · Haryana

Invest in agricultural technology

Anand Agro Farms advances practical application of agricultural technology on ancestral land. Focus remains on measurable reduction of water use, post-harvest loss, and input waste while maintaining soil health. Work includes horticulture pivot, zero-waste biomass utilisation, and supporting digital tools for planning and compliance.

Explore precision farming techniques

Precision methods under evaluation include soil-moisture sensing, targeted nutrient placement, and simple data logging of crop cycles. These support the shift from conventional paddy-wheat rotation toward higher-value horticulture and biomass utilisation.

  • Soil and crop monitoring with locally serviceable sensors
  • Targeted input application to reduce waste
  • Post-harvest handling aligned with KhetSe zero-waste processing

Location

Kheri Shish Gram, Pehowa, District Kurukshetra, Haryana. Ancestral farmland under transition to high-value horticulture. Daily operations and research continue from this base.
